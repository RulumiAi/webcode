# Prohibited Content Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

This Policy provides detailed rules for Content created, generated, uploaded, stored, or shared through the Lurova Services. It supplements the Community Guidelines and Terms of Service.

Lurova is limited to users aged 18 or older. Lawful, fictional, consensual adult Content may be permitted, but the categories below are prohibited in public and private areas of the Services.

## 1. Child sexual exploitation and sexualized minors

We prohibit:

- child sexual abuse material and child sexual exploitation material, whether photographic, illustrated, animated, or AI-generated;
- sexual, fetishized, or exploitative depictions of a person who is under 18, is described as under 18, or reasonably appears to be under 18;
- sexualized "aged-up" depictions of identifiable minors;
- grooming, sexual extortion, or attempts to obtain sexual material from a minor;
- instructions, links, coded terms, or other material used to locate, create, trade, conceal, or distribute such content; and
- attempts to evade age-safety systems or describe a minor as an adult while retaining clearly minor characteristics.

## 2. Non-consensual sexual content

We prohibit sexual Content involving actual or simulated:

- rape, coercion, threats, blackmail, abduction, trafficking, or forced marriage;
- unconsciousness, sleep, intoxication, hypnosis, mind control, or another condition that removes meaningful consent;
- revenge pornography or distribution of intimate material without authorization;
- sexual deepfakes or sexualized use of a real person's face, body, likeness, or voice through a general user feature, regardless of claimed authorization; and
- eroticized abuse, captivity, torture, or loss of agency.

Labels such as "fantasy," "roleplay," or "fictional" do not make sexualized non-consent permissible.

## 3. Sexual exploitation, trafficking, and prostitution-related facilitation

We prohibit Content or conduct that recruits, advertises, grooms, transports, purchases, sells, controls, or otherwise facilitates the commercial sexual exploitation or trafficking of a person. This does not prohibit good-faith prevention, reporting, academic, legal, news, or victim-support material that does not facilitate abuse.

## 4. Other prohibited sexual content

We prohibit:

- incest involving immediate family members or equivalent familial roles;
- sexual activity involving real animals or realistic animal abuse;
- necrophilia;
- sexualized gore, mutilation, torture, or severe bodily harm;
- sexual content involving human waste, vomit, or comparable extreme bodily-fluid fetishes where it is graphic, exploitative, or likely to cause serious harm; and
- content used to advertise or arrange unlawful sexual services.

Fictional adult Content outside these categories may still be limited in public, discoverable, image, audio, or video features.

## 5. Serious violence and dangerous conduct

We prohibit credible threats, incitement of serious violence, instructions that materially enable violent wrongdoing, sexual violence, torture presented for gratification, and graphic depictions created primarily to shock, degrade, or celebrate suffering. Non-graphic fictional violence, news, documentary, educational, historical, and prevention content may be permitted depending on context.

## 6. Terrorism, violent extremism, and organized crime

We prohibit recruitment, propaganda, praise, fundraising, operational support, or instructions for terrorist or violent extremist activity and serious organized crime. Neutral reporting, counterspeech, research, and prevention material may be permitted.

## 7. Self-harm, suicide, and eating disorders

We prohibit Content that instructs, pressures, challenges, or encourages a person to harm themselves, or that romanticizes severe self-harm or eating disorders in a way likely to increase risk. Recovery, prevention, supportive conversation, and responsible fictional treatment may be permitted.

## 8. Hate and targeted abuse

We prohibit advocacy of hatred, dehumanization, exclusion, or violence based on protected characteristics; credible threats; stalking; doxxing; and severe or persistent targeted harassment. Discussion, criticism, satire, and fictional conflict are assessed in context.

## 9. Real-person deception and privacy abuse

We prohibit deceptive impersonation, identity theft, unauthorized voice cloning, intimate deepfakes, disclosure of sensitive personal information, and Content designed to mislead others about a real person's actions or statements. General users may not submit visual depictions of actual sexually explicit conduct involving real performers. Any future verified-performer feature would be governed by separate consent, age-verification, recordkeeping, and safety requirements. Clearly disclosed non-sexual parody is not automatically prohibited, but it must comply with privacy, defamation, harassment, and intellectual-property law.

## 10. Crime, controlled goods, and professional harm

We prohibit Content that materially facilitates fraud, theft, hacking, weapons trafficking, unlawful drug production or distribution, evasion of law enforcement, or other serious criminal activity. General educational information, harm reduction, fictional storytelling, and high-level discussion may be permitted when they do not meaningfully enable wrongdoing.

The Services must not be used to impersonate a licensed professional or to provide dangerously misleading medical, legal, financial, or emergency instructions.

## 11. Intellectual property and confidential information

We prohibit unauthorized distribution of copyrighted material, trade secrets, private communications, stolen data, or other material that infringes enforceable rights. Rights holders may submit a removal request under the Content Removal Policy.

## 12. Fraud, spam, malware, and security abuse

We prohibit phishing, scams, payment fraud, deceptive promotions, malware, credential theft, spam, unauthorized scraping, denial-of-service activity, and attempts to bypass or probe safety and security systems without authorization.

## 13. Enforcement and appeals

We use automated systems, human review, and user reports to enforce this Policy. Actions may include generation blocking, visibility limits, removal, feature restrictions, suspension, or termination. We consider severity, intent, context, recurrence, and the risk of harm.

You may request review under the Complaint Policy. We may preserve and report suspected illegal Content when required or permitted by applicable law.

## 14. Contact

Report suspected violations using an available in-product reporting tool or email soulmingleai@outlook.com. Provide enough information to locate the Content, but do not send illegal imagery or unnecessary identity documents.
