# Complaint Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

This Policy explains how users and other affected persons may raise concerns about the Lurova Services and request review of certain decisions.

## 1. What you may complain about

Complaints may concern:

- account access, service quality, subscriptions, or billing;
- Content that may violate the Community Guidelines or Prohibited Content Policy;
- an account restriction, Content removal, or other moderation decision;
- privacy, data protection, intellectual property, or unauthorized use of identity or likeness;
- fraud, security, trafficking, child safety, or another safety concern; or
- the handling of an earlier report or complaint.

Refund requests are also subject to the Refund Policy. Privacy requests are also subject to the Privacy Policy.

## 2. How to submit a complaint

Use an available in-product reporting or support tool, or email soulmingleai@outlook.com.

Where relevant, include:

- the email address associated with your account;
- the URL, username, character, message, transaction, or other information needed to locate the issue;
- a clear description of what happened and the outcome you seek; and
- screenshots or documents that are lawful and necessary to understand the issue.

You do not need to provide your full legal name or a government identity document for an ordinary safety report. Do not email passwords, full payment-card details, illegal imagery, or unnecessary identity documents.

## 3. Urgent safety reports

Reports involving suspected child sexual exploitation, trafficking, non-consensual intimate imagery, a credible threat, or imminent danger are prioritized. If someone is in immediate physical danger, contact the local emergency services or appropriate local authority. Lurova is not an emergency service.

We may temporarily restrict Content or an account while an urgent report is reviewed.

## 4. Acknowledgment and target times

We aim to acknowledge complaints within 72 hours.

We aim to resolve most non-urgent complaints within 10 business days after receiving the information reasonably needed to investigate. Complex matters, legal requests, cross-border issues, or cases involving multiple parties may take longer. If a matter is materially delayed, we will provide a status update where practical.

These are service targets, not guarantees, and they do not limit any shorter period required by applicable law.

## 5. Investigation and outcome

We may review relevant account records, Content, moderation signals, transaction information, and prior communications. Access is limited to what is reasonably necessary and is handled under the Privacy Policy.

Possible outcomes include:

- no action where no violation is found;
- an explanation or correction;
- restoration, restriction, or removal of Content;
- a warning, feature restriction, suspension, or account termination;
- a refund or account credit where eligible under the Refund Policy;
- changes to a safety control or process; or
- preservation or disclosure of information where required or permitted by law.

Where practical and legally permitted, we will explain the outcome and the policy basis. We may withhold details that would expose another person's personal data, reveal security methods, endanger a person, or interfere with an investigation.

## 6. Requesting review

If you disagree with a moderation, removal, or account decision, you may request review within 30 days after notice. Explain why you believe the decision was incorrect and provide any relevant context.

The review will be conducted by a person or process that is not solely dependent on the original automated decision. The outcome may affirm, reverse, or modify the original decision. Repeated requests presenting no new information may be closed.

## 7. Australian online-safety complaints

Users in Australia may use this process to make an online-safety complaint. Where Australian law or an applicable industry code provides a specific right to request review of a decision concerning Class 1A or Class 1B material, that request may be submitted within the legally required period, including within one month where applicable. Mandatory rights to contact the eSafety Commissioner or another regulator are not limited by this Policy.

## 8. Fair treatment and confidentiality

We do not retaliate against a person for making a good-faith report or complaint. Knowingly false, abusive, or fraudulent complaints may themselves violate the Terms of Service.

Complaint information is shared only with personnel, service providers, advisers, counterparties, or authorities who reasonably need it for investigation, safety, legal compliance, or dispute resolution.

## 9. External rights

This Policy does not prevent you from contacting a regulator, payment provider, court, law-enforcement agency, or other body where you have a legal right to do so. We encourage users to contact us first when practical, but doing so is not a condition for exercising mandatory rights.
