# Sex Trafficking and Sexual Exploitation Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Lurova prohibits the use of its Services to facilitate, promote, normalize, or profit from human trafficking, sex trafficking, or the sexual exploitation of another person. This Policy applies to AI-generated Content, user-created Content, uploads, communications, profiles, and account conduct.

## 1. Prohibited conduct

You must not use the Services to:

- recruit, advertise, groom, transport, harbor, control, purchase, sell, or arrange the exploitation of a person;
- threaten, coerce, deceive, blackmail, isolate, or otherwise control a person for sexual exploitation;
- advertise or solicit a minor or a person whose age is unclear for sexual activity;
- share contact, travel, payment, location, or operational information for the purpose of trafficking or exploitation;
- romanticize or eroticize trafficking, captivity, forced prostitution, or commercial sexual exploitation;
- identify or target vulnerable persons for exploitation; or
- evade detection, reporting, or law-enforcement activity concerning trafficking.

## 2. Permitted public-interest context

Good-faith prevention, victim-support, legal, academic, documentary, news, and educational Content may be permitted when it does not expose a victim, facilitate exploitation, or include prohibited sexual Content. Fictional storytelling is assessed in context, but sexualized non-consent and romanticized trafficking are prohibited.

## 3. Detection and response

We may use automated detection, trained reviewers, payment and account signals, and reports from users or trusted organizations to identify suspected exploitation. Detection tools are not conclusive and may produce false positives.

When we identify a credible risk, we may:

- restrict access to Content or an account while reviewing it;
- remove prohibited Content;
- suspend or terminate an account;
- preserve relevant records;
- take steps to protect an affected person; or
- disclose information to competent authorities or specialist organizations when required or permitted by law.

We do not automatically report a user solely because automated systems flag Content. Decisions are based on available evidence, urgency, applicable law, and the need to protect affected persons.

## 4. Reporting

Report suspected trafficking or sexual exploitation using an available in-product reporting tool or by emailing soulmingleai@outlook.com. Provide the location of the Content and any non-graphic information that may help us assess urgency. Do not confront a suspected trafficker, redistribute illegal material, or put yourself or another person at risk.

Urgent reports are prioritized and may receive immediate protective action. If a person is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or an appropriate anti-trafficking authority. Lurova is not an emergency service.

## 5. Privacy and victim protection

We limit access to trafficking reports and avoid disclosing a reporter's or victim's identity unless necessary for safety, investigation, legal compliance, or with valid authorization. Information is handled under the Privacy Policy.

## 6. Review

An affected user may request review of an enforcement decision under the Complaint Policy, except where review or disclosure is restricted by law or would create a material safety risk.
