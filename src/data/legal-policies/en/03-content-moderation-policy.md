# Content Moderation Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

This Policy explains how LUDORIA STUDIO PTE. LTD. moderates Content and conduct on the Lurova Services. It should be read with the Community Guidelines, Prohibited Content Policy, Privacy Policy, and Complaint Policy.

## 1. Scope

Moderation may apply to prompts, generations, uploads, AI characters, profiles, public posts, private chats, images, audio, video, account activity, and reports. The fact that Content is private, fictional, or AI-generated does not exempt it from safety rules.

We do not promise that every item is reviewed before or after it appears. Safety systems can make mistakes, and Content that is initially available may later be restricted or removed.

## 2. How moderation works

We may use:

- automated classifiers, filters, hash matching, rate limits, and abuse-detection systems;
- review by trained personnel or authorized service providers;
- reports from users, rights holders, trusted organizations, and authorities;
- targeted review of public, widely distributed, trending, or high-risk Content; and
- investigation of suspicious account, payment, or security activity.

Automated systems may block a request, limit a feature, flag Content for review, or reduce distribution. A flag does not necessarily mean that a violation occurred.

## 3. Review of private Content

Private chats and unpublished Content may be processed by automated safety systems. Human access is limited to circumstances such as:

- reviewing Content flagged as potentially involving serious harm or a policy violation;
- investigating a user report, appeal, security incident, or technical problem;
- complying with a valid legal obligation; or
- conducting controlled quality and safety evaluation under access restrictions.

Access must be limited to authorized personnel with a legitimate need. The Privacy Policy explains the purposes, disclosures, and rights associated with this processing.

## 4. Priority risks

We prioritize reports and signals involving:

- child sexual exploitation or grooming;
- trafficking or imminent sexual exploitation;
- credible threats or imminent danger to life;
- non-consensual intimate imagery;
- terrorism or violent extremist activity;
- severe self-harm risk;
- compromised accounts or active fraud; and
- legal orders requiring prompt action.

We may immediately restrict access while an urgent matter is assessed.

## 5. Enforcement principles

We aim to make decisions that are proportionate, consistent, and sensitive to context. Relevant factors may include:

- the nature and severity of the Content or conduct;
- whether harm is actual, imminent, or reasonably foreseeable;
- whether the Content is public or private and how widely it was distributed;
- intent, including attempted evasion;
- prior violations and account history;
- public-interest, educational, prevention, or documentary context; and
- applicable legal requirements.

## 6. Possible actions

We may:

- block or modify a generation request;
- add warnings, age controls, or reduced visibility;
- remove or disable Content;
- prevent re-upload of confirmed prohibited material;
- restrict access to particular features;
- issue a warning;
- temporarily suspend or permanently terminate an account;
- preserve relevant evidence; or
- disclose information to competent authorities when required or permitted by law.

Permanent termination is generally reserved for severe violations, repeated violations, deliberate evasion, or conduct presenting substantial legal or safety risk.

## 7. Notice and review

Where practical and legally permitted, we will notify an affected user of a material action, identify the policy basis, and explain how to request review. We may delay or omit details where disclosure could expose a victim, reveal security methods, interfere with an investigation, or violate law.

A review request should normally be submitted within 30 days after notice. Review will be performed by a person or process not solely dependent on the original automated decision. The Complaint Policy provides further details.

## 8. Reporting

Reports may be submitted using available in-product tools or by email to soulmingleai@outlook.com. Include enough information to locate the issue. Do not download, attach, or redistribute suspected child sexual abuse material or other illegal imagery.

We aim to acknowledge ordinary complaints within 72 hours and to resolve most non-urgent matters within 10 business days. Complex cases may take longer; when appropriate, we will provide a status update. Urgent safety reports are prioritized and may be acted on immediately.

## 9. Accuracy and continuous improvement

We evaluate moderation performance and may update rules, models, reviewer guidance, and product controls. We do not guarantee that automated systems will detect every violation or avoid every false positive. Material policy changes will be communicated as described in the Terms of Service.
