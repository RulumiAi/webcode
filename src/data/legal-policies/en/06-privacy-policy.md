# Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LUDORIA STUDIO PTE. LTD. ("Lurova," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal data in connection with lurova.ai and the related applications, features, and services (collectively, the "Services").

For purposes of applicable data-protection law, LUDORIA STUDIO PTE. LTD. is the organization responsible for the processing described in this Policy unless another notice states otherwise.

## 1. Scope and age requirement

The Services are intended only for persons aged 18 or older. They are not directed to children. If we learn that a person under 18 has provided personal data, we will take appropriate steps to restrict the account and delete or otherwise handle the data as required by law and safety obligations.

## 2. Personal data we collect

### 2.1 Information you provide

We may collect:

- account details, such as email address, username, password credentials in protected form, age-confirmation information, and settings;
- profile information and Content, including prompts, chats, characters, descriptions, images, audio, video, and uploads;
- support, complaint, appeal, and safety-report information;
- purchase, subscription, refund, and transaction information, excluding full payment-card details processed directly by payment providers;
- consent choices, marketing preferences, and communications with us; and
- identity or authorization information when reasonably necessary for a rights, safety, or fraud request.

Please do not submit unnecessary sensitive information. Do not send government identity documents unless we have specifically requested proportionate verification and provided suitable instructions.

### 2.2 Information collected automatically

We and our service providers may collect:

- IP address, device and browser information, operating system, language, and approximate location derived from IP address;
- log, diagnostic, crash, security, and fraud-prevention data;
- pages, features, characters, and controls used, and the time and duration of interactions;
- cookie and similar-technology identifiers; and
- subscription status and transaction results received from payment providers.

### 2.3 Information from third parties

We may receive information from login providers, payment processors, fraud-prevention providers, analytics providers, app stores, and other services you choose to connect. Their processing is also governed by their own privacy terms.

## 3. How we use personal data

We use personal data to:

- create and administer accounts;
- provide requested AI characters, generations, communications, and other features;
- process subscriptions, payments, refunds, and account credits;
- personalize settings and improve reliability and usability;
- operate content moderation, age-safety, security, abuse-prevention, and fraud controls;
- investigate reports, complaints, legal claims, and suspected violations;
- communicate service, security, policy, and support information;
- send marketing where permitted and subject to available choices;
- evaluate and improve models, systems, and features as described below;
- comply with legal obligations and protect users, the public, and our rights; and
- create aggregated or de-identified information that is no longer reasonably linked to an individual.

## 4. Legal bases

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we rely on one or more of:

- performance of a contract, including providing an account and requested Services;
- legitimate interests, such as securing, moderating, maintaining, and improving the Services, balanced against individual rights;
- consent, including for optional marketing, certain cookies, or model-improvement uses where consent is required;
- compliance with legal obligations; and
- protection of vital interests or other grounds available under applicable law.

Consent may be withdrawn for future processing where consent is the legal basis. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

## 5. Chats, Content review, and AI improvement

Prompts, private chats, uploads, and generations are processed to provide the requested Services. Automated safety systems may analyze public and private Content. Authorized personnel or service providers may access limited Content when necessary to investigate a report, review a safety flag or appeal, resolve a technical issue, conduct controlled safety and quality evaluation, or comply with law.

Private chat Content will not be used to train a general-purpose AI model unless:

- the user has affirmatively enabled or consented to that use where required; or
- the Content has been processed so that it is no longer personal data under applicable law.

Public Content and feedback may be used to evaluate, improve, and promote the Services in accordance with the Terms of Service and applicable settings. Identity documents, payment credentials, and material submitted solely for a privacy, safety, or removal request are not used for AI model training.

We may use human reviewers for carefully controlled evaluation. Access should be limited, logged where appropriate, and subject to confidentiality and security requirements.

## 6. Automated decisions

Automated systems may block a generation, flag Content, limit a feature, or identify suspected fraud. Significant account actions are subject to the review process described in the Complaint Policy where required by law or provided by us. You may contact us if you believe an automated decision was incorrect.

## 7. Disclosure of personal data

We may disclose personal data to:

- hosting, cloud, content-delivery, communications, customer-support, moderation, analytics, security, and technical service providers;
- payment processors, app stores, fraud-prevention providers, and financial institutions;
- professional advisers, auditors, insurers, and corporate affiliates;
- a buyer, investor, lender, or successor in connection with a proposed or completed corporate transaction, subject to appropriate safeguards;
- competent authorities, courts, regulators, or other parties when required or permitted by law; and
- another party at your direction or with valid authorization.

We require service providers to process personal data only for authorized purposes and subject to appropriate contractual and security obligations.

## 8. International transfers

Personal data may be processed in Singapore and other countries where we or our providers operate. Where required, we use recognized transfer mechanisms and safeguards, such as contractual protections, transfer assessments, or other measures accepted under applicable law.

## 9. Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose collected, including to provide the Services, maintain security and moderation records, complete transactions, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and comply with legal obligations.

Retention periods vary by category. Relevant factors include account status, user deletion requests, legal limitation periods, financial-record requirements, safety risks, backup cycles, and the need to prevent repeat abuse. When data is no longer required, we delete, aggregate, or de-identify it using reasonable processes.

Deletion from active systems may not immediately remove data from security backups. Backup copies are isolated from ordinary use and removed or overwritten according to applicable backup cycles, unless preservation is legally required.

## 10. Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies for essential functions, security, preferences, analytics, and, where offered, advertising or measurement. Where required by law, non-essential technologies are used only after an appropriate choice is presented. You may also use browser or device controls, though blocking essential technologies may affect functionality.

## 11. Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect personal data, including access controls, secure development and transmission practices, monitoring, and incident-response procedures appropriate to the nature of the data and risk. No system can guarantee absolute security.

If you believe your account or data has been compromised, contact us promptly and change affected credentials.

## 12. Your rights and choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

- access or obtain a copy of personal data;
- correct inaccurate personal data;
- delete personal data;
- withdraw consent or object to certain processing;
- restrict processing;
- receive portable data;
- opt out of certain marketing, targeted advertising, sale, sharing, or model-improvement uses where applicable;
- request review of certain automated decisions; and
- complain to a data-protection authority.

To exercise a right, email soulmingleai@outlook.com. We may take proportionate steps to verify the request. Authorized agents may be required to show valid authority. Rights are subject to legal exceptions, including safety, fraud prevention, freedom of expression, legal claims, and recordkeeping obligations.

Users may unsubscribe from marketing using the link in the message or available account controls. Service and security messages may still be sent where necessary.

You may also contact the Personal Data Protection Commission of Singapore where you have the right to do so.
