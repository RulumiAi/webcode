# Pre-Screening and Post-Screening Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

This Policy explains how Lurova may assess Content before and after it is generated, uploaded, displayed, stored, or shared. It supplements the Content Moderation Policy.

## 1. Content covered

Screening may apply to:

- prompts and AI-generated text, images, audio, and video;
- uploads and attachments;
- character names, descriptions, tags, and profile fields;
- public posts and private chats;
- usernames and relevant account signals; and
- other Content or activity presenting a safety, security, or legal risk.

## 2. Pre-screening

Before Content is generated or made available, automated systems may:

- detect known or likely prohibited patterns;
- block, limit, or request modification of a prompt or upload;
- apply age, visibility, or feature restrictions;
- identify suspected security abuse or attempts to evade controls; or
- route a limited category of Content for human review.

Pre-screening does not mean that Lurova approves, endorses, or has manually reviewed Content. A system may fail to detect a violation or may incorrectly restrict permissible Content.

## 3. Post-screening

After Content is generated or made available, we may use automated detection, user reports, targeted audits, and human review to identify violations that were not detected earlier or that become apparent through context, distribution, or later information.

Public and widely distributed Content may receive additional review. Private Content may be processed as explained in the Content Moderation Policy and Privacy Policy.

## 4. Human review

Human review is limited to authorized personnel or service providers with a legitimate need. Review may occur for flagged high-risk Content, user reports, appeals, quality and safety testing, security incidents, or legal compliance. Access controls, confidentiality duties, and retention rules apply.

## 5. Actions

Screening may result in:

- no action;
- a generation block or request to revise Content;
- a warning, age gate, or reduced visibility;
- temporary restriction pending review;
- removal or disabling of Content;
- account restrictions; or
- escalation under the Content Moderation Policy.

## 6. High-risk matters

Signals involving child sexual exploitation, trafficking, non-consensual intimate imagery, credible threats, imminent self-harm, terrorism, or active fraud may be restricted immediately and prioritized for review. We may preserve and disclose information when required or permitted by law.

## 7. Challenges and feedback

Safety systems are imperfect. Users may request review of significant restrictions under the Complaint Policy. We use reports, appeals, testing, and expert input to improve accuracy and reduce both missed violations and incorrect restrictions.

## 8. Contact

Questions and reports may be submitted using available in-product tools or by email to soulmingleai@outlook.com.
