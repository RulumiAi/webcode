# Refund Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

This Refund Policy applies to purchases made directly from LUDORIA STUDIO PTE. LTD. for the Lurova Services. It does not limit any refund, cancellation, withdrawal, or consumer right that cannot lawfully be excluded.

## 1. Subscriptions

You may cancel a subscription through available account controls or by contacting soulmingleai@outlook.com. Cancellation stops future renewal charges. Unless applicable law or this Policy provides otherwise, access continues until the end of the paid billing period and the unused portion is not automatically refunded.

Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal where practical to allow processing. If a renewal occurs after a timely cancellation request because of our error, contact us for review.

## 2. Service defects and billing errors

You may request a refund or correction if:

- you were charged more than once for the same purchase;
- a charge resulted from our verified billing error;
- a paid feature was materially unavailable or materially different from its description for a significant part of the paid period; or
- applicable consumer law gives you a refund or other remedy.

Submit the request promptly and, where practical, within 14 days after the charge or discovery of the issue. This request period does not reduce a longer mandatory legal period.

Depending on the circumstances, the remedy may be a full or partial refund, restoration of service, extension of access, replacement, or account credit. Mandatory legal remedies are not subject to our discretion.

## 3. Change of mind

Except where a statutory withdrawal right applies, purchases generally are not refundable solely because you changed your mind, did not use the Services, or no longer want the subscription after the paid period began.

## 4. EEA and United Kingdom withdrawal rights

Consumers in the European Economic Area or United Kingdom may have a statutory right to withdraw from certain distance contracts within 14 days. The availability and loss of that right depend on whether the purchase is a service or digital content and on the consent and acknowledgment collected at checkout.

Where required, we will not treat the withdrawal right as lost unless the legally required express request, consent, and acknowledgment were obtained. Nothing in this Policy replaces the mandatory information or model withdrawal form required by applicable law.

Users in Switzerland and other jurisdictions retain the withdrawal and consumer rights provided by their local mandatory law. This Policy does not claim that every jurisdiction provides the same 14-day right.

## 5. Credits, tokens, and promotional value

If the Services offer credits, tokens, or similar virtual value:

- promotional or free value has no cash value unless required by law;
- used or expired value is generally not refundable;
- unused purchased value may be refunded where required by law or where the underlying transaction is eligible under this Policy; and
- virtual value may not be transferred or redeemed outside the Services unless expressly stated.

## 6. App-store and third-party purchases

Purchases made through Apple, Google, or another third-party marketplace are also subject to that provider's billing and refund process. We may be unable to issue a direct refund for a transaction controlled by the third party. We will provide reasonable information needed to support an eligible request.

## 7. Cryptocurrency or alternative payment methods

If an alternative payment method is offered, technical irreversibility or price volatility does not exclude mandatory consumer rights. An approved refund may be issued through a method reasonably selected to comply with law, payment-network requirements, and fraud controls. The refunded amount will not include speculative gains caused by exchange-rate changes unless required by law.

## 8. Chargebacks and payment disputes

You may exercise chargeback or payment-dispute rights available from your payment provider. We encourage you to contact us first when practical so we can investigate, but doing so is not a condition for exercising a mandatory right.

We may temporarily restrict an account while investigating credible evidence of unauthorized payment, account compromise, or payment fraud. We will not suspend an account merely because a user made a good-faith payment dispute.

## 9. How to request a refund

Email soulmingleai@outlook.com with:

- the account email address;
- transaction date and amount;
- order or transaction reference, if available;
- the reason for the request; and
- any relevant non-sensitive supporting information.

Do not send a password or full payment-card number. We aim to acknowledge requests within 72 hours and resolve most requests within 10 business days after receiving the necessary information. Approved refunds may take additional time to appear, depending on the payment provider.

Refunds are normally returned to the original payment method unless that is impossible, unlawful, or otherwise agreed.
