# Payments Integrity and Anti-Fraud Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

This Policy explains how Lurova addresses payment abuse, account fraud, sanctions risk, and suspected money laundering in connection with the Services. It does not state that Lurova is a regulated financial institution or that it has obligations that legally belong to a payment provider.

## 1. Roles and payment providers

Payments may be processed by independent payment providers, app stores, banks, or digital-asset providers. Those providers may perform identity, fraud, sanctions, anti-money-laundering, and transaction checks under their own legal obligations and terms.

Lurova may use information received from those providers and its own account and transaction signals to protect users, prevent loss, enforce the Terms of Service, and comply with applicable law.

## 2. Prohibited activity

You must not:

- use a payment method without authorization;
- use stolen credentials or compromised accounts;
- manipulate refunds, promotions, virtual value, or chargebacks;
- conceal the source, ownership, destination, or purpose of unlawful funds;
- structure or route transactions to evade payment, sanctions, fraud, or legal controls;
- transact for or on behalf of a sanctioned person where prohibited by applicable law;
- resell accounts, subscriptions, or virtual value without authorization; or
- use the Services to facilitate fraud, money laundering, trafficking, or another unlawful transaction.

## 3. Risk indicators

Signals may include unusual transaction velocity, repeated failed payment attempts, inconsistent account or payment information, known compromised credentials, suspicious device or network patterns, abnormal refund activity, and information received from payment providers or authorities.

A risk signal is not conclusive proof of wrongdoing. We may seek additional information or review the circumstances before taking final action, except where an immediate restriction is reasonably necessary.

## 4. Actions we may take

Where reasonably necessary, we may:

- decline or delay a transaction;
- require additional authentication through a payment provider;
- temporarily restrict a payment method or account;
- cancel fraudulently obtained credits or benefits;
- refund an unauthorized transaction where appropriate;
- preserve records relevant to an investigation;
- suspend or terminate an account for a confirmed serious or repeated violation; or
- disclose information to a payment provider, financial institution, regulator, or competent authority when required or permitted by law.

We may be legally prohibited from telling a user about certain investigations or reports.

## 5. Sanctions and geographic restrictions

We may restrict transactions or access where required by sanctions, export-control, payment-network, or other applicable legal requirements. Restrictions are based on the laws and provider obligations applicable to the transaction, not a blanket assertion that every person in a particular country is prohibited.

## 6. Account compromise and unauthorized payments

If you believe your account or payment method was used without authorization, contact us promptly and also contact the relevant payment provider or financial institution. Change compromised credentials and preserve transaction references. We may temporarily restrict the account to prevent further loss.

## 7. Reviews and payment disputes

You may request review of an account or payment restriction by emailing soulmingleai@outlook.com. We may ask for proportionate information needed to confirm account control or transaction authority. Do not send full payment-card details or unnecessary identity documents.

Good-faith use of a lawful chargeback or payment-dispute process is not itself fraud. Knowingly false disputes, repeated abuse, or use of unauthorized payment methods may result in enforcement.

## 8. Privacy

Payment and fraud information is handled under the Privacy Policy. We limit collection and disclosure to what is reasonably necessary for payments, security, legal compliance, and dispute resolution.
