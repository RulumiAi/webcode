# Community Guidelines

Last updated: September 1, 2026

These Community Guidelines apply to the website, applications, and related services operated by LUDORIA STUDIO PTE. LTD. under the Lurova name, including lurova.ai (collectively, the "Services"). They apply to prompts, private chats, public posts, AI characters, profiles, images, audio, video, uploads, and other content created, generated, stored, or shared through the Services ("Content").

Lurova is an adults-only creative platform. All users must be at least 18 years old. Lawful fictional content involving consenting adults may be permitted, including adult romantic or sexual themes, but only within the limits below and any controls made available in the Services.

## 1. Core expectations

You must:

- use the Services lawfully and honestly;
- respect the safety, privacy, dignity, and intellectual property of others;
- provide accurate age and account information;
- keep your account secure and not permit anyone under 18 to use it; and
- comply with these Guidelines, the Terms of Service, and the Prohibited Content Policy.

## 2. Content that is never allowed

The following Content and conduct are prohibited, whether real, fictional, AI-generated, uploaded, public, or private.

### 2.1 Child sexual exploitation and minor sexualization

Do not create, request, upload, possess, share, or promote:

- child sexual abuse material or child sexual exploitation material;
- sexual or fetishized depictions of anyone under 18, described as under 18, or reasonably appearing to be under 18;
- "aged-up" versions of identifiable minors presented in sexual contexts;
- grooming, sexual extortion, or instructions that facilitate the exploitation of minors; or
- attempts to evade these rules through coded language, altered spelling, roleplay, or ambiguous age descriptions.

When age is unclear, Content may be restricted or removed until it can be assessed safely.

### 2.2 Non-consensual sexual content and exploitation

Do not create or share sexual Content involving coercion, force, incapacitation, unconsciousness, trafficking, abduction, sexual blackmail, loss of agency, or any other absence of meaningful consent. This includes non-consensual intimate imagery and sexual deepfakes involving a real person.

Fictional conflict or dark themes may be allowed outside a sexual context, but sexualized non-consent is prohibited even when described as fictional or roleplay.

### 2.3 Trafficking and commercial sexual exploitation

Do not use the Services to recruit, advertise, groom, transport, control, sell, purchase, or otherwise facilitate the sexual exploitation or trafficking of any person. Content that meaningfully assists such conduct is prohibited. Good-faith prevention, reporting, education, news, and victim-support content may be permitted when it does not expose victims or facilitate abuse.

### 2.4 Serious violence, terrorism, and criminal facilitation

Do not use the Services to threaten or incite serious violence, praise or recruit for terrorist or violent extremist organizations, facilitate organized crime, or provide operational instructions that materially enable serious wrongdoing. Graphic violence may be restricted where it is gratuitous, exploitative, or presented for sexual gratification.

### 2.5 Self-harm and dangerous behavior

Do not encourage, instruct, pressure, or romanticize suicide, self-harm, eating disorders, or other conduct likely to cause serious physical injury. Supportive discussion, recovery content, prevention, and non-graphic fictional treatment may be permitted.

### 2.6 Hate, harassment, and targeted abuse

Do not promote hatred or violence against people based on protected characteristics. Do not engage in credible threats, stalking, persistent targeted harassment, doxxing, or coordinated abuse. Criticism, satire, and discussion of public issues may be permitted when they do not cross into threats, dehumanization, or targeted harassment.

### 2.7 Real-person impersonation and intimate content

Do not deceptively impersonate a real person or create sexual or intimate Content using a real person's identity, likeness, or voice. General users may not submit visual depictions of actual sexually explicit conduct involving real performers. Any future feature involving verified real performers would require separate consent, age-verification, recordkeeping, and safety controls. Clearly disclosed non-sexual parody may be permitted unless it is defamatory, harassing, or otherwise unlawful.

### 2.8 Privacy and intellectual property

Do not publish personal data, private communications, intimate material, copyrighted works, trademarks, or other protected material unless you have the right to do so. Do not use the Services to identify, track, or expose a private individual without a legitimate and lawful reason.

### 2.9 Fraud, spam, and platform abuse

Do not use the Services for scams, phishing, deceptive commercial activity, malware, credential theft, payment fraud, spam, unauthorized scraping, security attacks, or attempts to bypass technical or safety controls.

## 3. Adult fictional content

Adult romantic or sexual Content may be permitted only where:

- every depicted or described participant is clearly an adult;
- participation is consensual and no prohibited powerlessness or exploitation is sexualized;
- no actual real performer or real person's likeness is used in sexual Content through a general user feature;
- the Content does not involve incest, bestiality involving real animals, necrophilia, trafficking, or other categories prohibited by the Prohibited Content Policy;
- the Content is displayed only in areas and to audiences permitted by applicable product controls and law; and
- the Content otherwise complies with these Guidelines.

Permission for adult fictional Content is not a guarantee that every generation will be available. We may apply additional restrictions to images, audio, video, public profiles, discoverable characters, or payment-supported features.

## 4. Context and public-interest uses

Context matters. Content concerning abuse, crime, extremism, self-harm, sexuality, or other sensitive subjects may be allowed for news reporting, education, research, prevention, condemnation, recovery, or artistic treatment when it does not facilitate harm and complies with applicable law. We may add warnings, reduce distribution, or restrict access instead of removing such Content.

## 5. Enforcement

Depending on severity, context, prior violations, and risk, we may:

- block a prompt or generation;
- add a warning or age gate;
- limit visibility or recommendations;
- remove or disable Content;
- restrict features;
- temporarily suspend or permanently terminate an account; or
- preserve and disclose information to competent authorities when required or permitted by law.

Where practical and legally permitted, we will provide notice of a significant enforcement action and information about how to request review. We may act without prior notice where necessary to protect a person, preserve evidence, comply with law, or address an urgent security risk.

## 6. Reporting and review

You may report Content or conduct using any reporting tool made available in the Services or by emailing soulmingleai@outlook.com. Please include the relevant URL, username, character, message, or other information needed to locate the issue. Do not email illegal imagery or unnecessary identity documents.

If you believe we made an enforcement error, you may request review under the Complaint Policy. Reports made in good faith will not result in retaliation.
