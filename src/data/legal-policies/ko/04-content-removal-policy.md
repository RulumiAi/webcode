# Content Removal Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

This Policy explains how a person may request removal or restriction of Content on the Lurova Services. Content on the Services may be AI-generated, user-created, uploaded, or derived from multiple sources. Lurova does not assume that every resemblance to a real person is accidental and reviews each request on its facts.

## 1. Grounds for a request

You may request review of Content that you reasonably believe:

- uses your identity, image, voice, personal data, or intimate material without authorization;
- deceptively impersonates you or creates a harmful false impression that you created, endorsed, or participated in the Content;
- infringes copyright, trademark, or another enforceable right;
- contains confidential information, doxxing, harassment, threats, or non-consensual intimate imagery;
- violates the Community Guidelines or Prohibited Content Policy; or
- is otherwise unlawful in a jurisdiction applicable to the Content or Services.

## 2. How to submit a request

Use an available in-product reporting tool or email soulmingleai@outlook.com. Include:

- the URL or information needed to locate the Content;
- the reason you believe it should be removed or restricted;
- your relationship to the person or rights involved; and
- any lawful supporting material reasonably necessary to assess the request.

Do not send illegal imagery. For suspected child sexual abuse material, provide only the location of the Content and any non-graphic identifying information.

## 3. Identity and authority checks

We use proportionate verification. We will not normally require a government identity document where account control, a matching email address, a public professional profile, or other less intrusive evidence is sufficient.

If stronger verification is reasonably necessary to prevent fraud or protect another person's rights, we will explain what is needed and how it should be provided. You should redact document numbers and unrelated information unless they are specifically required. Do not send identity documents to a general mailbox unless we have given you case-specific instructions for a suitable transmission method.

An urgent safety restriction does not depend on completing identity verification where the available information shows a credible risk of serious harm.

## 4. Interim action

While reviewing a request, we may temporarily limit access, discovery, sharing, or generation of the disputed Content when necessary to reduce a credible risk involving:

- child safety;
- non-consensual intimate imagery;
- trafficking or sexual exploitation;
- a credible threat or severe harassment;
- disclosure of highly sensitive personal data; or
- another serious and time-sensitive legal or safety concern.

## 5. Assessment

We may consider:

- the nature, source, and context of the Content;
- whether it identifies or closely depicts a real person;
- whether valid consent or authorization exists;
- the public interest, including legitimate news, criticism, research, or parody;
- the severity and likelihood of harm;
- the rights of the uploader, creator, subject, and public; and
- applicable law and platform policy.

## 6. Outcomes

We may:

- remove or disable the Content;
- restrict discovery, sharing, or generation;
- apply a warning, label, or age restriction;
- reject the request with an explanation where practical;
- ask for additional information;
- preserve relevant records; or
- take action against an account under the Content Moderation Policy.

When technically and legally appropriate, we may use matching or other controls to reduce repeated upload or regeneration of confirmed prohibited Content. We do not guarantee that every copy or similar generation can be identified or removed from third-party services.

## 7. Notice and competing rights

Where practical and legally permitted, we may notify the uploader or affected account and allow a response or appeal. We may withhold a requester's identity and other details where disclosure is unnecessary or could create risk. We may decline to notify a user where the Content involves child exploitation, non-consensual intimate imagery, trafficking, an urgent threat, a confidential investigation, or a legal prohibition on notice.

## 8. Copyright and trademark claims

Rights holders should identify the protected work or mark, the disputed Content, the basis of ownership or authority, and a good-faith explanation of the claimed infringement. We may request the statements or information required by applicable law and may provide a counter-notice process where appropriate.

## 9. Data handling

Information submitted with a removal request is used to assess the request, prevent abuse, maintain safety records, resolve disputes, and comply with law. Verification material is not used for advertising or AI model training. It is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes or as required by law, as further described in the Privacy Policy.

## 10. Timing and review

We prioritize urgent risks. For ordinary requests, we aim to acknowledge receipt within 72 hours and resolve most matters within 10 business days after receiving the information reasonably needed. Complex matters may take longer.

You may request review of an outcome within 30 days under the Complaint Policy.
