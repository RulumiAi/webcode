# 18 U.S.C. Sections 2257 and 2257A Notice

Last updated: September 1, 2026

This notice concerns the United States federal recordkeeping and labeling provisions in 18 U.S.C. Sections 2257 and 2257A and 28 C.F.R. Part 75. It is provided for transparency and does not declare that every item available through the Lurova Services is categorically exempt from every provision of those laws.

## 1. Nature of the Services

Lurova is an AI character and content-generation service intended only for users aged 18 or older. Content available through the Services may include AI-generated material, user prompts, user-created characters, and user uploads. We therefore do not represent that every item is created exclusively by Lurova or that every resemblance to a real person is necessarily accidental.

## 2. Real-person sexually explicit content

General users may not upload, create, or distribute visual depictions of actual sexually explicit conduct involving a real person. They also may not use a real person's identity or likeness in sexual Content, even with claimed consent, unless Lurova expressly offers a separate verified feature designed to satisfy applicable consent, age-verification, recordkeeping, and other legal requirements.

Sexual Content involving a minor, a person described as a minor, or a person who reasonably appears to be a minor is always prohibited, whether real, fictional, illustrated, animated, or AI-generated.

## 3. Application of Sections 2257 and 2257A

The application of Sections 2257 and 2257A depends on the nature, source, and production of particular Content and on the role of the persons involved. AI-generated or simulated Content that does not depict actual human beings engaged in actual sexually explicit conduct may fall outside particular recordkeeping requirements, but that conclusion must be assessed on the relevant facts and law.

Where Lurova is required to maintain records for particular Content, it will maintain and make those records available to the extent required by applicable law. Lurova may remove or restrict Content where its origin, age status, consent, or required records cannot be established.

## 4. User obligations

Users must not submit Content that falsely represents the age, consent, identity, source, or production circumstances of a depicted person. Users must cooperate with proportionate requests concerning age, consent, rights, and origin where necessary to assess compliance.

Submitting false information or attempting to evade these requirements may result in removal, suspension, termination, preservation of records, and disclosure to competent authorities where required or permitted by law.

## 5. Reporting

If you believe Content involves an actual minor, an adult presented as a minor, an actual real person or real person's likeness used sexually through a general user feature, or a visual depiction for which legally required records may be missing, report it using an available in-product tool or email soulmingleai@outlook.com. Provide the location of the Content. Do not download, copy, or attach suspected illegal imagery.
